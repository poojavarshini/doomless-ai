DOOMLESS AI — CHROME BETA

1. Unzip this download to a permanent folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
3. Turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder.
4. Open or reload Instagram Reels and pin DoomLess AI from the Extensions menu.

Privacy: DoomLess AI reads supported Reel metadata only after analysis is enabled or requested.
